TripSpend

Travel budget app for iPhone

TripSpend tracks what you spend on a trip, in any currency, and tells you how much you can spend today to stay on budget. It works offline.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?
On your iPhone, with a private backup in your own iCloud account. We don't run servers and never see your data.

Does it work without internet?
Yes. Logging, conversions and totals all work offline using the last exchange rates you synced. Tap "Sync latest rates" in Profile when you're online.

How is "left to spend today" calculated?
Budget left divided by days left, for the whole trip and for the city you're in. The lower of the two is shown.

I reinstalled the app. Where are my trips?
Make sure iCloud is turned on for your Apple Account (Settings → your name → iCloud). Your trips download automatically, which can take a minute.
