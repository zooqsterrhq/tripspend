Privacy Policy

TripSpend: Travel Budget App · Last updated October 24, 2026

TripSpend is made by BARAKAH APPS INC. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: we don't collect any of it.

Information we collect

None. TripSpend has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We don't run servers that receive your data.

Where your data is stored

  • On your device. Trips, cities, expenses, categories and settings are stored on your iPhone.
  • In your iCloud. If iCloud is turned on, Apple's CloudKit keeps a private backup in your own iCloud account so your trips survive a reinstall or new phone. Only you can access it; we can't. Apple's privacy policy applies to iCloud.

Exchange rates

When you tap "Sync latest rates", the app downloads public exchange rates from open.er-api.com (ExchangeRate-API). The request contains no personal information or trip data.

Notifications

Morning, night and weekly summaries are created and scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

Deleting your data

Delete trips in the app, or delete the app. To remove the iCloud backup, go to Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → TripSpend.

Children

TripSpend doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
